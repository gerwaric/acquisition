## What this is

`brainstorming-notes/28-search-ruling-packet.md` turns note 31's
reconciliation into candidate decision lines for `decisions/search.md`.
Most lines harvest a note-31 row with the correction your stage-4 check
attached to it (K1–K7), and are marked *checked*. A few are marked
*chosen here*: your check said stage 5 "must choose and state" them,
and nobody had, so Fable chose. Those choices, and the one-page model in
§1 that Fable wrote to see whether the lines cohere, have been read by
one model only. That is what this check is for.

This is a check of the packet, not a new seat, a third proposal, or a
ruling. Under decision 15 (note 23) every line but the three ruled ones
is provisional until the owner's first seat at a usable surface.

## Check

1. **Each line marked *chosen here*** (C92's binder, sort scalar and
   "together" count; C93's undecided rule; C95's missing-value rule;
   C98's basis; C48's open SQL home). For each: is the choice stated
   completely enough that two builders would build the same thing; does
   it contradict any *checked* line, any K, or note 22's floor; is there
   a concrete counterexample, in the form your K1 and K6 used. Where the
   choice is one of several defensible ones, say which you would make
   and why — marked as your reading, one voice.
2. **§1, the model page.** Does every sentence trace to a row or a K, or
   carry its **new** mark; do the lines in §2 and the page describe one
   model; is anything on the page that no line rules, or ruled by a line
   that the page omits. The page is not harvested; its job is coherence.
3. **Each *checked* line.** Is it the row it names, with its K applied
   and nothing beyond — or has the harvest added, dropped or softened
   something. Name the sentence.
4. **Form.** Each line is one bullet: the ruling, *Why:* in a sentence,
   evidence, date. Does any still carry mechanism that belongs in §2b or
   in code; is any cited `S` id or `C` id wrong; does any line say more
   than its evidence supports.
5. **The parking lot.** Is each trigger one a build can recognise when
   it fires; is anything parked that a line already needs.

The counting rules of note 23 hold: a preference for your own model's
design is weak evidence, a counterexample is strong whichever design it
lands on, and two lines resting on one text are one piece of evidence.
