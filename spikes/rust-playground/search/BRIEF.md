One thing sets this field apart from everything `pseudo.` holds. A
total adds lines up; this field works back from the number an item

answer by that line: it gives another roll, and a confident number.

where what the item shows gives one value from lines whose side is
established, and undecided anywhere else, the reason saying which part

## What is built

**The reference data, two things, reviewed and shipped as the class
table is** (C68, C106, which names base ranges as data): generated by
one script under `tools/` from the owner's pinned RePoE clone and the
committed extracts the track's scripts read, with `--check`, the header
citing source and commit, a version on every answer's basis.

- *The ranges*: for each base its name and the minimum and maximum of
  each defence it ranges, and nothing else of the export. It is
  `search/repoe/data/base-defences.csv` row for row — 476 rows, 474
  names, Two-Toned Boots held three times, two bases whose minimum is
  their maximum, one ranging no defence, as
  `percentile-expected.py` prints them: show that yours is the same.
- *The defence lines*: each text a local defence stat displays, with
  what it does — a flat addition or an increase, to which defences —
  and whether a global stat displays the same text (a twin text). 22
  texts, 10 of them twin, as the same script prints them, the enchant
  that drops the quality term one of the 22. The `reduced` spelling is
  its stat below nothing.

The realms the tables cover are the class table's.

**The field**, a named pure function in `pseudo.rs`, as the paragraph
and J5, J6, J8 and J33 to J35 state it; `percentile-expected.py`'s
`read` is those sentences as code, each step citing its own, and is
evidence of the reading, never the build's source. What the paragraph
leaves to this brief:

- *It reads lines.* The module doc says a derived field reads
  properties and never lines; this one reads both, and the sentence is
  yours to change. It reads the item's marks too — `searing`,
  `tangled`, `synthesised` — as the deriver holds them.
- *J29 is not its rule.* A total leaves `crucibleMods` out and is never
  opened by it. This field is undecided on a defence line there (J33),
  and an array it reads that is no array leaves it open.
- *Unknown of no more than was lost* (rule 8). A line of a type no
  roll reads opens nothing, whatever stat it is. A mark is asked only
  of an implicit twin text, so a mark that is no yes or no opens the
  field there and nowhere else. A flag opens nothing (J32).
- *The arithmetic is exact.* A displayed number, an increase and a
  quality are fractions, never floats: the run that wrote the table
  found three items of the copy a float read wrongly, a product at an
  exact half landing under it. Look for the maker that exists
  (`exact.rs`) before writing one (rule 10).
- *The value is a whole number*, the average rounded half up, over 100
  where a roll is over the base's maximum and below 0 where one is
  under its minimum (J34). It takes no slot word.
- *Lacked* is an item, in a realm the tables cover, that displays none
  of the four defences. In a realm they do not cover every item is
  undecided, one displaying no defence among them (J35).

**The definition, printed** by `--describe`, as every computed value's
is: the site's rule in the site's words; its one departure, the exact
half (J5); how a twin text is told and the captured lines each part
rests on — 17 eldritch implicits left out, 442 explicit and 10
synthesised implicits the item's own, counted by
`search/pseudo-stats/scripts/defence-twins.py` (the paragraph's 421 is
of an earlier day); that a roll out of the base's range still reads,
which no capture has shown (J34), so a line misread can show a number
out of range; where it is undecided (J33), a crucible defence line
among them as not yet asked of the site; that a defence bound on the
site is inexact (C102); the sources and version.

**`has:` with a slot word on a ranged total.** `has:pseudo.<ranged>.avg`
is refused today as an unknown name, and offers the right reading. The
reading stays; the refusal is of the kind that is its own — a slot word
where none is taken — and never `UnknownName`, with its test. Whether
an `ErrorKind` (`error.rs`) already says that or one is added is yours. Whether `low` and
`high` behind `has:` are the same refusal is yours to find in the
code and report: they are no stop.

## The work, in order

1. **The tests, before any code, each seen to fail for the reason you
   expect.**
   - *The table.* One test reads `percentile-expected.csv` where it
     lies, unchanged, and puts each of its cases into a fixture store
     through the store's own ingest: the realm, the base, the rarity,
     the marks, the properties as the row displays them, and its lines
     in the arrays and under the flags the row gives — a line entered
     as the row gives it, a mod of several rows as one entry, never
     taken apart (the record's shape of fault "A claim the code did not
     make"). The field is then asked of each case: lacked; or its
     value, by comparison and by the row; or undecided, the cause
     pinning what could not be established. The `site` and `agrees`
     columns are evidence and assert nothing.
   - *The four worked cases*, their arithmetic already by hand in the
     table: asked by comparison, by `has:`, sorted, and summed in a
     count.
   - *Undecided under `not` and `or`*, worked by hand from C93 and one
     of the table's J33 cases: the not of a comparison on the field,
     and an or of it with a term that holds.
   - *What does not open it*: a line of a type no roll reads on an
     item of neither mark; an unread flag; an unread mark on an item
     with no implicit twin.
   - *The refusal* of `has:` with a slot word, by its kind.
   - One CLI `--json` smoke for a captured hybrid and one for an item
     that lacks the field, and `--describe` of the field.
2. **The reference data and its generator**, `--check` passing.
3. **The reading.**
4. **The module docs.** `pseudo.rs` carries the paragraph's rules and
   J5, J6, J8 and J33 to J35 under C101, the owner's quoted words
   verbatim ("keep reads over 100"; "undecided"), so that the session
   can take the paragraph out of `search/DESIGN.md` at the close.
5. **Three breaks.** Break the build three times, one rule each — one
   of them J8's eldritch implicit, one the exact half, one a J33 kind —
   and name the test that noticed. A break nothing notices is a
   finding, reported, not fixed in silence.
6. **The copy.** Over the copy as M3 asks it (`search/MEASUREMENTS.md`,
   the M3 block; the release build): the items that have the field,
   those that lack it and those undecided by cause, against
   `percentile-expected-copy.csv`; and M3's warm number for one ask
   that compares the field, beside the same ask without it. A
   measurement, reported with its command, never a test. The copy is
   local and may be absent: say so and go on.
7. **The gate**, all seven commands, in `AGENTS.md`'s order, after the
   last edit; build again after `cargo fmt` changes a file.
