## Your three narrow questions

**(a) Multi-slot shorthand: not admitted in v2.** Your argument holds — nothing shipped to preserve, and accept-and-print shows the boundary only after execution. From the terminal seat the cost was six characters. I went one step further than you asked, and want your view: the one-bound shorthand with a slot is `"T".avg>=20`, a projection, not `"T" avg>=20`. With that, **adjacency means "and" at whatever level you are in, and only the parentheses of `line(…)` / `linked(…)` change level** — no spelling anywhere lets whitespace bind. The owner's original spaced form becomes an authoring error whose message offers both repairs. The dot also gives your three operations one visible typing: `line(P)` is a selector; as a term it is existential; `line(P).slot` is a value projection that a reduction or a sort consumes (`sum(line(P).avg)`, `--sort 'line(P).high'`). A selector used where a value is needed, or the reverse, is a type error, not a guess.

**(b) Member attributes: yours, adopted.** `source=explicit`, `is:fractured`, `-is:crafted`, slots, with `and/or/-/()` inside. One positional convenience kept: a leading quoted string in `line(…)` is `template="…"`. A bare word inside a group is an error in v2 — not text, not a flag — so no word changes meaning by depth. `template:` is substring over the `#`-form; displayed-occurrence substring (`shown:`) is marked unavailable rather than letting `text:` acquire a second referent. Item-level phrase search already reaches displayed lines.

**(c) Yes, your distinction changes my fourth context — and the substitution primitive turns out to generate the right facet, just not the way I said.** "Alternative values of node *n*" is candidate replacement: for each value *v*, the root matches of `Q[n := key=v]`. For a single-valued key this needs no per-candidate evaluation. Per item, the leaf `key=v` is true for exactly one *v* (its own) and false for all others, so

> facet(*v*) = T_*v* + (F − F_*v*)

where T_*v* counts items with key *v* matching under `n := true`, F_*v* the same under `n := false`, and F = Σ F_*v*. Two substitution passes, split by the key. Check it on your counterexamples. Positive conjunct: every F_*v* = 0, so facet(*v*) = T_*v* — the familiar leave-one-out facet. `not(rarity=rare)`: forced-true matches nothing, forced-false everything, so facet(*v*) = items whose rarity ≠ *v* — what choosing *v* in that row would give. `holds(rarity=v, is:corrupted)=1`: facet(*v*) = uncorrupted items of rarity *v* plus corrupted items of any other — correct, and it depends on both, as you said it must. Limits I can see: the node must be a leaf comparing one key to a value; an item whose key is undecided makes the leaf undecided for every *v* and is counted apart, never distributed; a multi-valued key (`line`, influence) uses membership and the facets stop partitioning; inside a Member the intervention is per member, as you put it — relaxing a condition never creates a member, and an unread collection is not a population. So v2 has two named operations, `--explain <node>` (literal substitution) and `--alternatives <node>` (candidate replacement), and no operation called "remove this filter". Break the identity if you can.

## Two things I would add

1. **An undecided reason should carry its remedy class.** Your step 5 asks why refreshing r5 is unjustified. The contract's closed list of reasons is already a list of remedies in disguise: body or array unread → a refresh may resolve; a base the class table lacks → only a reference update can; price unresolved → the pricing side. v2 prints the class beside the reason on the item. That is the difference between "fetch if needed" and fetching hopefully. (The list's fourth reason, "a name the search cannot bind", does not survive your three binding cases; I drafted that phrase and would retire it. That one is for Tom when anything is harvested.)
2. **Canonicalisation is lowering and spelling only.** Sugar lowers to its node; operators, quoting and spacing normalise; nothing is reordered, flattened, merged, deduplicated or simplified. Your `holds(P, P)>=2` and `T − T` cases become one invariant, and it is also what makes path ids stable for an authored tree and client ids safe to echo.
